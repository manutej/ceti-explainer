CETI Explainer · Episode 01

Local truths, global maps

Sheaves, operads, and cohomology without a seminar voice. One city. Many maps. A thread that either holds or does not.

A map is not the city

A neighborhood map is a local truth: good on its own street, silent about the next one. Category theory is the craft of keeping those local truths honest when they meet. Things, and arrows between them. If two arrows can be followed in sequence, they compose. That is almost the whole subject.

Sheaves glue. Cohomology leftover.

A sheaf is many maps covering one city. On every overlapping street they must agree. If they do, they glue. Restriction says looking closer must not change the story. If one map bends the river left and the other right, there is an obstruction. Cohomology names that leftover disagreement. It diagnoses. It does not pardon.

Operads nest

A category composes in a line. An operad composes in a nest: bowls in bowls, recipes in recipes. Many inputs, one output, substitution in any slot.

Why an agent already lives here

Retrieve, edit, test, commit: five patches on one city. If they disagree on the file, the test, the order, they do not glue. The harness fails at the seam, not in the neighborhood.
