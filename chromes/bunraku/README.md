# F · Bunraku — who holds the rods

**Little Worlds, staged honestly.** A toy theatre of cut kozo paper at true depths, one raking tungsten lamp per
stage, a real perspective camera with per-plane depth of field. The agent is not a character. It is a puppet that
cannot move on its own, worked in plain sight by three operators in black: the model plans (copper rod), tools act
(slate rod), the harness checks (sage crook). One stage is one run, one move is one step, and a stage goes dark where
its puppet fell.

## Levels
**Glance (built, revision 1).**
- **Shared (35 s).** Title, anatomy, one performance with a single caught slip that is visibly redone. The commit
  is chalked on a closed curtain. The same run then falls unchecked. Fifty stages run twice on the same draws: a fallen
  stage's curtain closes and its slip marks the move on a 1–20 board. The film ends on a curtain call over a chalk
  ruler (19 white strokes plus 22 sage = 41; expectation notches ± sd; peach strokes for the moves made again).
- **Native (34.5 s).** The person walks and slips too (sketch), then hands the plank to a puppet and takes the check
  rod. Fifty stages are checked by one person per stage (41 lit), per three (26), and per row (19, sketch model). The
  film ends on one person's rods stretched across a row.

**Wield (live page).**
- **Shared:** where does the check operator stand? Each choice re-runs the district on the same draws and counts the
  moves made again. Checking only at the end gives 26 lit and 480 moves redone. Checking every step gives 41 lit and
  33 moves redone.
- **Native:** how many stages can one person check?

**Grasp (spec, 2 min).**
- **The Trace** opens on one stage. Each move hangs a paper tag: the plan (copper), then `fetch_purchase_orders` and
  its result (slate), then the match.
- At move 7 the tag reads *"Acme Corp" ≠ "ACME Corporation"*. The puppet slips and the crook catches it. The next tag,
  *retry by tax ID*, passes.
- Pull back: the stage becomes one box of 2,000 on a hillside, and the arch tally takes over.
- Two honesty beats follow:
  - Stages wired to one lamp circuit share a fuse. The errors are correlated, and checks barely help.
  - A redo counter shows that checks cost time.

**Master (spec).** An explorable district: set p, c, k and gates, wire lamp circuits, spend a staff budget.

## Craft
- **Shadows:** analytic, with penumbra proportional to the gap, computed in a pass at one third of the resolution.
- **Depth of field:** read from an atlas pyramid that is blurred once.
- **Falling slips:** driven by the APW plate ODE.
- **Render cost:** about 0.4–0.9 s per frame, using eight small shaders.

## Known weaknesses
- At district scale the puppet is a warm dot. The address reads only in the medium shot.
- Native: "one person per row" is subtle in the wide shot; the count carries it (sketch model).
- The drape folds are regular, and most of the paper relief is lost at 960×540.
- The bald paper head can read as a mannequin.
