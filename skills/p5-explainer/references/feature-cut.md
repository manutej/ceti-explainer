# Feature cut on p5 — cadence, mapping, crit checklist

## Movement → what p5 can own (from the Type-safe AI build)

| Movement | Job (LONGFORM) | p5 earns it with | SVG always owns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | brand assembly + promise | the mark from N counted marks converging from a full-frame field; last stragglers make the eye an event | title, streaks, promise line |
| 2 Core idea | one-sentence model + scored demo | marks (characters, items) that are loose, then snap to the structure | the input, the reply, stamps, labels |
| 3 Mechanism | where it comes from | conserved mass re-seating along the structure's own paths; powers-of-ten zoom on a large field | every number, token, label, the lockup |
| 4 Scale | why size matters | a train of N countable runs thinning by a rate; piles as a visible staircase | counter (from the shared canonical function), arithmetic foot |
| 5 Process | step loop + live demo | pour along couplers; spill at a break | lanes, stamps, signatures |
| 6 Limits | honesty, equal weight | a measured physical effect (a moiré of two pitches 1 % apart) | the paired cards |
| 7 Land | quotable line, mark reforms | the same marks reform the mark | the line, end card |

## Rules learned in review

1. **Scale for 1080p.** Headline 40, mono 15 (min 13), sans 18, counters 40 — in a 960×540 viewBox. Fill the Body; ≤ ~45 % empty frame.
2. **One canonical function per shared number** (`TS.runsArrived`): the SVG counter and the p5 pen read it, so the film never disagrees with itself.
3. **Load stays on rails.** Mass moves along the structure's own geometry, never as free spray.
4. **Make size the event.** Fields of 10⁵ marks read as grey at 1080p; zoom from a legible close-up to the whole.
5. **Rings and stamps never cross text** — grow them out of their own label.
6. **Types and claims audited** — every signature that "matches" must match (`out === in`), every cross-model number says it is two models, every claim of "checked" vs "precomputed" says which.
7. **Interactions re-run the film's own maths** (`constrain`, `u`, `stepRate`), jump to the moment they change, and never break scrubbing.

## Crit checklist (give to the art-director and pedagogy seats)

- Is the p5 cut distinguishable from the SVG cut in a contact sheet at thumbnail size? Where does p5 earn its place?
- Per movement: emptiest frame, weakest payoff, any >3 s lull, any overlap.
- Every on-screen number against the brief; captions aligned with what is on screen at that second.
- Would a smart non-specialist name the mechanism after one viewing?
