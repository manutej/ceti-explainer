## P1 · "Worse overall", in three dimensions   (the hard chain)

Subject: Simpson's paradox, reusing factory/topics/simpsons (claims verified). Why 3D: the paradox IS a change of
viewpoint. 4,526 applicants as boxes; seen from the front the men's column is taller (45 % vs 30 %); the camera flies
to the side and the six department slabs show women ahead in four of six. Chain: arsenal/patterns/webgl-scene
(baked boxes, keyed camera slerp, extruded headline via textToModel), morph-type (headline numbers morph), camera
(keyed moves, log zoom), materials/shader neon post for the reveal frame only. Brand ceti-boardwalk-dark; chrome
none; level manager (neon post is not exec-clean, by Q6). Format case (60–75 s + card).

Success criteria: gate PASS (G1–G10) on the selected draft; three drafts visibly different on the contact strips;
evaluator findings expressed only as knob and caption edits; two fix rounds converge (fewer findings each round);
seconds per frame under 1.5 s headless; total agent cost recorded.

Measurements recorded in factory/films/simpsons-3d/PROTOTYPE.md: wall time per stage, tokens per agent,
findings per round, gate rows per draft, s/frame, bytes.
