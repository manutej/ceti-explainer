  followed orbit (ellipse, ten lit dots, and from 58.5 s its spin arrow labelled "SPIN, HOW MUCH THIS ORBIT SWIRLS") gives
  "spin" a picture before the word is used (strip-10 cells 9-11).
- The Earth fixture is a lay picture for every word: distance is a lit radius, speed is an arrow, and "multiply" is the
  terms flying into "distance x speed = ?" (strip-08 cells 2-11, 44-47.5 s). The run is then repeated for the farthest point
  and the two results are stacked (strip-09/10, 49-56 s). Counts come before ratios: the first ratio is 0.011 % at 53.6 s,
  after 3,000 at 26 s.

  M1 (60-68 s) and M2 (88-96 s) and come back after a settle of 1.5 s or more (R5, T3). Every new Earth number holds 2.5 s
  (34, 36.5, 39, 41.5 s), and the products stay on stage after their captions. Drift at 103.5 and 121 s is ambient,
  at about 1 degree per second. M2 lands nearly flat (el 0.5), which suits a P1 re-stack landing.
- The honest line is on stage at 141.6-150 s and the card comes last (152 s).

  - The network pins 0.00095 and "about 250 times" are missing (126-131.4 s). Leader knob as an experiment, else scope.
  - Three captions wrap (captions 2, 31 and 37: 12.4-19.8, 114-118.8 and 135.3-140.8 s).

## Verdicts
- a: the pictures carry the collapse, the layers and Earth's equality, so a viewer can follow the argument from the picture. The shells and the network
  ratio are still told rather than shown. Promote.

  camera moves break the grammar. Borrow its captions and its axis-word idea.
