## Ranking

### 1. c "the instrument" (turntable), the winner
- Front view reads "men ahead" (strip-05/06, 26-35.5 s). The lavender men's column is taller and lit higher than
  the coral women's column, and it holds for 9 s with the count heads 1,198 and 557 at 28 units.
- The turn is shown as one event. In strip-08, 41-45 s, the same boxes fly off the two columns into six pairs of
  slabs on the platter, so "same boxes" is shown on screen.
- The side view shows the reversal on screen (strip-08/09/10, 45.5-58.5 s). Six pairs stand in a row. Every slab is
  normalised to the same height, so the lit share is the admission rate. A call-out walks A to F, and each pair
  leaves its rates (62% 82%, 63% 68%, 37% 34%, 33% 35%, 28% 24%, 6% 7%) in a ledger above the row. The ledger stays
  up, so by 54 s the viewer can count four women-higher pairs on one frame without reading a caption. This is the
  only draft where a manager can get the reversal from the pictures alone (P1).
- The neon reveal lands as one beat at 55.5-57.5 s: the extruded "2 OF 6" turns, the glow peaks and then clears.
  The YOU 5 / TRUTH 2 strip then places the committed number against the truth. neonGain 0.45 lights the numeral,
  not the whole room.
- In MONDAY (strip-11/12, 63-72 s) the hook headline returns with KILLED struck through, plus "Same mix of leads?",
  which brings the opening back. There is one honest-limits caption, and the CETI card comes last.
- Faults, all fixable in round 1:
  - The stale overall "30" sits over the side view from 44 to 46.5 s.
  - The reveal caption arrives at 54.2 s, about 1.3 s before the picture.
  - The mix caption gives 51 % / 7 % at 58.6 s, before the counts appear at 58.8 s.
  - The rate ledger is at its 20-unit floor.
  - Each department holds only about 0.4 s between morphs (48.5-50.5 s).
  - The turntable ring crosses the caption line in HOOK and MONDAY (strip-01, strip-12).

### 2. a "the city" (iso street of slabs)
- It has the cleanest front view of the three (strip-05/06): coral and lavender columns, lit bottom up, with count
  heads. "Men ahead" is clear.
- The side view does not show the reversal. The iso camera puts the women's row in front of the men's row
  (strip-08/09, 45.5-56.5 s), so the two lit heights of a pair are never side by side, and the tall thin women's A
  tower hides the men's slabs behind it.
- Pairs step every 1.0 s against a 0.55 s morph. A, B, C and F are half-formed in about half the frames (46.5, 47.5,
  48.5, 50.5 s), and nothing persists once the next pair arrives.
- The reveal at 52.5 s says "2 OF 6", and the MEN +3 / MEN +4 pointers at C and E are good. But "women ahead in four"
  is never visible.
- The hook headline "Converts worse overall." collides with the OLD PROCESS label from 2 to 7 s (strip-01/02).
- The neon is a moment (52.5 s), and the city is handsome.

### 3. b "the exhibit" (gallery, rail dolly)
- The grey room is murky: towers are dark on dark (strip-04/05).
- The front towers light in department strata (26.5-35.5 s), so "men ahead" reads as stripes, not as a taller
  lit column.
- In the side view the boxes are stacked by count, not normalised (strip-08/09, 46-56 s). Height means applicants,
  not rate, so the women's A pile is a low tray that looks behind even at 82 %.
- The close tracking shots let one slab hide the next: D covers C at 52-53 s, and E covers F at 54-56 s.
- The reversal is then told by the banner "WOMEN HIGHER IN 4 OF 6" (57.5-62 s) over a small, far wide shot. The neon
  at 58.5-60 s is a wash over every lit box.
- KILLED overprints "overall." from 4.5 to 8 s.
- Strengths: the biggest, best-ranked headline numbers, and pair holds of 1.9 s.

## Verdicts (one line each)
- **c**: wins. It is the only draft whose side view shows "women ahead in four of six" as a picture, and its faults
  are all timing and size knobs.
- **a**: best front view and a real neon moment, but the iso occlusion and 1 s pairs keep the reversal in the captions.
- **b**: strong type, but the reversal rests on count-stacked heights, occluding close shots and a banner.

## Beyond scope (could not be expressed as a knob or caption)
- Front-view count labels: the "APPLIED" / "ADMITTED OF 2,691" sub-lines sit on the column tops, and arriving boxes
  run through them (17.5-30 s). The labels need a fixed offset above the tallest column (film.js).
- KILLED stamp: in c it overprints the full stop of "Converts worse overall." (4.5-8 s, 63-72 s). The stamp
  position is not a knob.
- The small "45 %" / "30 %" labels at the column tops (36-40 s) duplicate the big headline and clutter the tops.
  They should go in film.js.
- a's MEN +3 / MEN +4 pointers on C and E at the reveal would make "worse in 2" point at its two pairs. That needs
  new marks in film.js; the numbers are already claims (gapC, gapE).
- The ledger underlines the higher rate of each pair, but the women-higher pairs are not tinted as a set at the
  reveal. A "count the four" pass (A, B, D, F lit together for about 1 s before the 2 OF 6) would make the reversal
  impossible to miss. That needs new timing logic, not a knob.
- claims.json `where` fields still name the old caption contents (e.g. gapE "caption 11"). This is cosmetic and was
  not edited (the evaluator may not touch claims.json).
