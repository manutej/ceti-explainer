## Verdicts
- **a**: the clean ink film that keeps every text rule. Its training slide and its tree swap are too faint, and both
  can be fixed with knobs. **Winner.**
- **b**: the more tactile pictures (the toy, the tree, the lit arcs), but it breaks the series' one-line and
  result-size rules, and puts a box, a grid and a light pool on a manager's stage.
