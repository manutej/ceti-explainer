Decisions inside the brief

  "MIDDLE THREE FIFTHS · SHOWN AT THEIR AVERAGE". Lit height = gain on one scale (knob gainFull, never shown); devs have their own scale.
- Director's choices followed: NBER figures, "skill fifth" wording, METR 2026 follow-up not on stage; the honest line also says the journal
  version rounds differently (one line, two wrapped rows, roled secondary 16, MONDAY 138-150).
