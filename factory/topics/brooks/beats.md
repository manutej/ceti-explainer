## THE COUNT, frame by frame (36.0 to 62.0)

### Geometry (design units)
- Ring centre C = (250, 262), radius R = 168. Slot k in 0..19 at angle θk = −90° + 18°·k:
  x = 250 + 168·cos θk, y = 262 + 168·sin θk. Dots r = 8. Ring spans y 94 to 430.
- Order people join (person index → slot):
  P1..P5 → 0, 4, 8, 12, 16 (a regular pentagon);
  P6..P10 → 2, 6, 10, 14, 18 (with P1..P5, a regular decagon);
  P11..P20 → 1, 3, 5, ..., 19 (all 20 slots).
- Path list, fixed order. Phase A (hook): pairs among P1..P5 lexicographic: (1,2) (1,3) (1,4) (1,5)
  (2,3) (2,4) (2,5) (3,4) (3,5) (4,5) = 10. Phase B: for j = 6..10, for i = 1..j−1: (i, j) = 35 paths
  (5 + 6 + 7 + 8 + 9). Phase C: for j = 11..20, for i = 1..j−1: (i, j) = 145 paths. Path m (1-based,
  global) gets tally tick m.
- A path line grows from Pi toward Pj: endpoint = lerp(Pi, Pj, p), p = clamp((t − start)/dur, 0, 1).
  It "lands" when p = 1; the PATHS counter = number of landed paths; tally tick m appears when path m
  lands.
- Lines: phases A and B 1.0 u ink at 85 % while settled; a line in flight and for 0.6 s after landing is
  red pencil. Phase C lines 0.6 u ink at 45 %, red while in flight. Draw lines on the p5 canvas; dots,
  counters, ticks and type in the SVG pool.
- Right column x 520 to 920. Counters: label "PEOPLE" at (520, 96) and "PATHS" at (720, 96), 14 u
  chrome; values 64 u mono at (520, 160) and (720, 160). Verdict slot: baseline y 214, 28 u (results
  never smaller than 28 u).
- Tally: origin (520, 250); 25 ticks per row, x pitch 15 u (row width 375); tick 2.5 u wide × 18 u
  tall; row pitch 26 u. Tick m sits at row floor((m−1)/25), column (m−1) mod 25. 190 ticks fill 7 full
  rows and 15 of row 8 (bottom edge y 450, clear of the caption band).

### Purity notes for the builder
All positions and times above are closed-form in t: no shuffle is needed (path order is the fixed
lexicographic order), so no RNG at all. Re-seek must clear `style` and text of hidden pooled elements.
