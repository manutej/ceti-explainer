  - A-shared and F-shared are both a dark wall of ~50 lit/unlit miniatures.

## 4. Fixes

**A Escapement**
1. **Stay in 3D.** Cut the flat grids at 14.2–32.4s and run all 20 ticks in the 12.0s tilted bank. A slipped movement freezes with a peach tooth and a dimmed dial. Put the checked bank *behind* in depth, not beside.
2. **End as a chart.** Re-rack by stop tooth; the silhouette is the survival curve. One engraved numeral replaces the flap boxes.
3. **Native.** Build a true involute train of varied sizes, with jobs falling *at the mesh*. Teach by adding handoffs (2→6→12) as the delivered bin shrinks.

**B Marbling**
1. **Shared 12.3–31.5s.** Use 12 full-bleed trays with no frames. Put a tick on each tray's pass ruler at the pass where the stray landed.
2. **Kill H1.** Make the strays CETI peach and drop the hairlines. Make the skimmer a paper strip that physically lifts the drop.
3. **Fix the honesty clash.** At 14.7s the film says "exact inverse"; at 19.2s, one noise gives three pictures. Show a visibly different ghost comb per prompt. Let the tray fill the frame at 2.5–14.7s.

**C Delta**
1. **Ruler.** At 22–31.5s, size the sediment cones by the failures at each weir (≈100 at weir 1 down to ≈38 at weir 20), at the angle of repose, under the faint exact curve.
2. **One river.** Make one meandering river that bifurcates into a bare channel and a braided one, sharing one sea. This replaces the stacked twin and puts the terrain to work.
3. **Native.** Move the landslide to weirs 1–3 and make it huge, with the whole day's grain dammed behind it. Use 4 rivers, not 8, and drop the title wipe.

**D Ledger**
1. **Re-seed.** Pick seeds within 0.5 sd of expected, or headline the expectation. "Without checks ≈ 0 h saved" *is* the lesson.
2. **Real pictograms.** Draw Arntz-grade invoices (folded corner, three rules) at 2× size. Show all 100 sliding at once along min-crossing paths into their exception rows; that is the impossibility moment.
3. **Silence.** Cut ~30 text items to a headline and totals; tool names on hover.

**E Margin**
1. **Make the 26.0s run-strips the hero.** Use 50 full-width rows; each is one stroke that stops at a peach cross. The pen then draws the envelope through the row ends.
2. **A real pen.** Show the nib, speed-dependent width, a re-dip blot and bleed on the circled 36 %. Write fewer, larger words.
3. **A second hand.** Write the guess in graphite at a different slant so the gap reads without words.

**F Bunraku**
1. **Wall (24.6–34.6s).** Use 20–25 larger stages. A failed stage's curtain closes to true black; a live stage keeps its raking light. Chalk the tally on the proscenium, large.
2. **Address.** Leave the dropped prop on the plank under a 1–20 move board.
3. **Look and title.** Swap the red velvet for the doctrine's black stage cloth. Fix the 1.8s sign ("W hat", "Al") and remove its ghost duplicate. End the native on its own image: one person's rods stretched across a row.

**G Run**
1. **Cloth, not a bar chart (24.8s).** Sort the columns by drop row and hang the swatch: the laddered hem is the survival curve, sagging with drape.
2. **Pull-back (18–21.2s).** Show one continuous sheen swatch with ladders as pale slits, not five barcode strips.
3. **Ground and type.** Use an undyed cream wool ground, which also separates G from C's umber. The 2.6s knitted "AI" reads as "AT". In the native, set the tokens horizontally on the stitches.

**H Exposure**
1. **Give the vertical axis a meaning.** Stack threads by the slit where they stopped, so plate density is survivors.
2. **The √N band is the hero.** Expose 10, then 100, then 2,000 on one plate over the faint exact field, with the band enlarged at 7.6–18.5s.
3. **Native.** The query *emits*, and its neighbours accumulate brightness by cosine. Open on the 23.5s "mouse" arc.

## 5. The edge

**G-shared, t = 5.80s.** Macro stockinette with true yarn shading: copper dropped loops in their columns and a sage hook mid-darn. The mark rule holds for every element (column = run, row = step, drop = slip, hook = check). The address is physical, SVG cannot reproduce it, and at 320 px it reads as knitting, not AI. Stunning, and clear without words.
