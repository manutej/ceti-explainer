# arXiv (source site)

## Definition
**arXiv** — the open preprint server hosting the HCI and AI papers on creative coding and explainer generation that this wiki cites; operated by Cornell University / authors. [S204]

Kind: **academic**; 9 registered sources, 0 rated primary. It is one of the source sites indexed in [[hub-sites]]. [S204][S205][S206]

## What it contributes
- Autocomplete was used in only about 12% of sessions [S204]
- The same study found that eager auto-refresh split students, and they wanted control over reruns [S352]
- LLM2Manim animations beat PowerPoint slides on adjusted post-test (83.4 vs 78.1, n=100 undergrads) [S287]
- TheoremExplainAgent achieved 93.8% video-generation success with o3-mini on 240 theorems, using Manim [S285]
