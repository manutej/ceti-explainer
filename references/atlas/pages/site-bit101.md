# BIT-101 blog (source site)

## Definition
**BIT-101 blog** — a community source at bit-101.com, credited to Keith Peters (BIT-101); the wiki uses it for “FOTD: loopsin”. [S135]

Kind: **community**; 1 registered source, 0 rated primary. It is one of the source sites indexed in [[hub-sites]]. [S135]

## What it contributes
- Dividing by total frames rather than total minus one avoids a duplicated frame at the loop point [S135]
- A looping value can be made by t = frame / totalFrames with zero-indexed frame, giving t in [0,1) [S135]
- Cosine mapped from (1,-1) to (min,max) makes a loop start and end at min, whereas sine starts mid-range [S135]
