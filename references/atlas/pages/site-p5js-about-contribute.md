# p5.js About, Contribute and Community pages (source site)

## Definition
**p5.js About, Contribute and Community pages** — the project-level pages of p5js.org (about, contribute, access, community, downloads, education resources) describing how p5.js is governed and extended; operated by Processing Foundation / p5.js. [S118]

Kind: **primary**; 7 registered sources, 7 rated primary. It is one of the source sites indexed in [[hub-sites]]. [S118][S197][S342]

## What it contributes
- Cassie Tarakajian created the p5.js Editor (2016–2022) [S342]
- FES contributors are asked to avoid figures of speech in messages [S347]
- Lifecycle order is presetup, postsetup, predraw, postdraw, remove. [S118]
- p5js.org lists Patt Vira as publishing weekly p5.js tutorial videos [S197]
