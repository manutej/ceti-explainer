# LGM 2026 p5.strands talk

## Definition
"Beginner-Friendly Shader Programming in p5.js v2" was a 20-minute Libre Graphics Meeting 2026 talk of 2026-04-22 on p5.strands API design, given by Pagurek, Plowden, Singh, Lim and Kuksenok [S50].

## Details
- The abstract says a strands shader modifies a default shader rather than specifying a full rendering pipeline, so authors need not know GLSL, uniforms or vertex/fragment stages initially [S50].
- Kit Kuksenok is listed as Processing Foundation Engineering Manager and p5.js Project Lead on the page; Perminder Singh is a GPU programmer and maintainer focused on the WebGL stack [S50].
- Another listing hosts the talk under Kuksenok at the Libre Graphics Meeting program page [S296].

## In explainer work
It is the clearest statement of why strands exists, and a source for how to teach shader edits to non-graphics audiences [S50].
