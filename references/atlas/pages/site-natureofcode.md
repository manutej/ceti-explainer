# The Nature of Code (source site)

## Definition
**The Nature of Code** — the open online edition of Shiffman's book, with p5.js examples on vectors, forces, oscillation and agents; operated by Daniel Shiffman. [S82]

Kind: **primary**; 6 registered sources, 6 rated primary. It is one of the source sites indexed in [[hub-sites]]. [S82][S83][S84]

## What it contributes
- The Nature of Code is free to read online, with p5.js examples [S179]
- NoC notes smaller noise increments (e.g. 0.01) give smoother output [S83]
- NoC builds waves by offsetting phase across many oscillating circles [S85]
