# Tyler Hobbs

## Definition
Tyler Hobbs is a generative artist and former software engineer, author of [[fidenza]] and of widely read craft essays on flow fields, colour and probability [S373][S365][S366].

## Details
- He says he works in Clojure, and his colour and subdivision essays use Quil/Clojure code [S373][S371][S372].
- Fidenza's shipped Art Blocks script uses p5-style calls, so "Fidenza is a p5 piece" is true of the artifact, not of his authoring environment [S367][S373].
- His flow-field essay recommends a grid margin of about 50% beyond the canvas, resolution about 0.5% of width and step length about 0.1–0.5% of width [S366].
- He calls the Gaussian his most-used distribution and recommends bounding it; he used a pseudo-Pareto distribution for polygon sizes [S370].
- He argues for HSB over RGB for artistic colour control [S371].
- He describes his process as discovery-driven, aimed Fidenza at a 99% quality bar, and has used pen plotters since 2016 [S368][S373].

## In explainer work
- His proportions make [[flow-field]] visuals scale cleanly with canvas size — essential for multi-resolution explainer renders (see [[resolution-independence]]) [S366][S367].
- His distributions and weighted palettes give controlled variety for B-roll and thumbnail series (see [[generative-distributions]], [[probabilistic-palette]]) [S370][S371].
