# Processing Foundation

## Definition
The Processing Foundation (PF) is the 501(c)(3) non-profit founded in 2012 by Reas, Fry and Shiffman that stewards Processing and p5.js [S345][S280].

## Details
- Since November 2024 PF has been led by executive co-directors Xin Xin (Community and Programs) and Roxana Hadad (Research and Education), with a stated renewed focus on youth [S279].
- Riot Games became a Gold Tier Fellowship supporter on 1 October 2026, with a Gaming Fellowship track starting in 2027; the Mark Cuban Foundation (29 Sep) and Jerome Foundation (30 Sep) were also announced [S280].
- No dollar amounts were disclosed for these backers [S280].
- PF says 100% of donations go to p5.js development, and more than 800 people have contributed to p5.js over 10+ years [S274][S47].
- 2026 was PF's 14th year in Google Summer of Code; GSoC 2026 projects include a translation tracker, Editor E2E tests and native SVG export [S63][S292].
- PF and the Tezos Foundation announced (14 Oct 2025) monthly p5.js 2.0 tutorials by generative artists paired with EditArt artworks [S298].

## In explainer work
- PF's programs ([[pf-fellowships]], [[oss-microgrants]], GSoC) fund many features explainer makers use — strands instancing, SVG export, translated references [S275][S292].
- PF's blog is the primary announcement channel for releases such as [[release-2-3]] [S274].
