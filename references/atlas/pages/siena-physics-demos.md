# Siena College physics demos

## Definition
The Siena College physics demos are interactive Processing/p5.js simulations, from projectile motion to neutrino oscillations, presented at the APS April Meeting 2022 [S222].

## Details
- Simulations expose adjustable parameters for students [S222].
- Evidence of effect is informal student feedback only [S222].

## In explainer work
- They show p5's fit for parameter-driven physics explainers, but offer no outcome data to justify interactivity over video (see [[p5-education-research]]) [S222].
