# Art Blocks (source site)

## Definition
**Art Blocks** — the generative-art platform's articles, project pages and artist docs; operated by Art Blocks. [S368]

Kind: **primary**; 6 registered sources, 6 rated primary. It is one of the source sites indexed in [[hub-sites]]. [S368][S369][S374]

## What it contributes
- Hobbs describes his process as discovery-driven rather than goal-driven. [S368]
- p5.js v1.0.0 is stored fully on-chain in the Art Blocks Dependency Registry. [S375]
- Ben Kovach started his practice with one generative sketch per day in October 2017. [S381]

## Reliability
- Coverage: 6 sources (6 rated primary, 0 secondary by the researchers); year range 2021–2021, 2 without a recorded date. [S368][S369][S374]
- Researcher caveat on S369: release 2021-06-11. This limits how far the wiki leans on the page [S369]
- Noted gap: **Ringers' and Archetype's libraries are unverified from primary sources.** Golid's p5 use comes only from a crypto news site, and the Art Blocks interview mentions only Processing. I found no source on Ringers' library. [S379]
- Noted gap: **No p5 2.x on Art Blocks.** The registry tops out at 1.11.11. 2.x features (async loadFont, textToContours) are not usable there as of this check. [S374]
- Treat this as mixed-grade evidence; prefer the primary p5.js documentation where the two disagree. [S368][S369][S374]
