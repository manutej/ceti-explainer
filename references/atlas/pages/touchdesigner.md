# TouchDesigner

## Definition

TouchDesigner is Derivative's visual development platform for realtime projects; the retrieved product page was thin, with no node, platform or licence detail. [S414]

## Details

- Its community site hosts a looping-noise tutorial recreating an Etienne Jacob sketch, with the tip to avoid doubling the last frame in GIF export. [S136]
- No sourced head-to-head against p5 for explainer animation was found. [S414]

## In explainer work

Treat it as the node-based, GPU-realtime alternative for installation-style work; for browser-shareable explainers p5 remains the lighter path (inference). [S414][S136]
