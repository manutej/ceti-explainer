# Processing Community Day

## Definition
Processing Community Day (PCD) is a set of community-led local events; PCD 2026 was recommended for October to mark Processing's 25th year [S297].

## Details
- The PCD @ Worldwide 2026 call for organizers was posted by Raphaël de Courville (sableraph), the Processing Community Lead, and lists about 58 local threads [S297].
- De Courville also relayed the staff statement on the 2026 Fellowship in the forum [S281].

## In explainer work
- PCD events are a venue for live explainer demos and for meeting the maintainers who own the features explainers depend on (inference from [S297]).
