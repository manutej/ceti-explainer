# Lauren Lee McCarthy

## Definition
Lauren Lee McCarthy is the artist and educator who created p5.js, serving as its Creator and Lead from 2013 to 2020 and as a mentor into 2021 [S342][S196].

## Details
- Work that became p5.js began in spring–fall 2013 as a [[processing-foundation]] fellowship, framed as an art and education platform prioritizing access and diversity [S346].
- She is a UCLA-affiliated artist working in social-technology performance, with fellowships including MacDowell and a Stanford HAI residency [S196].
- She co-authored the beginner book [[getting-started-with-p5js]] with Casey Reas and Ben Fry [S353].
- The p5.js About page lists her as Creator and Lead 2013–2020, then mentor 2020–2021; later leads include Qianqian Ye (since 2021) and Kit Kuksenok (since 2024) [S342].

## In explainer work
- The access-first framing she set at the founding fellowship explains why p5 ships screen-reader helpers and the Friendly Error System — features an explainer embed can use directly (see [[describe]], [[friendly-error-system]]) [S346][S342].
- Her "sketching" lineage from [[processing]] is why p5 favours a setup/draw immediate-mode model that explainer engines must wrap with a clock (inference from [S346]).
