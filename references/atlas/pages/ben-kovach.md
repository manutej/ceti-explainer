# Ben Kovach

## Definition
Ben Kovach is a generative artist whose Art Blocks project Edifice derives from his 2018 Good Night algorithm; he began with daily Processing sketches in October 2017 [S381].

## Details
- Edifice derives from the 2018 Good Night algorithm, and its palettes were sampled from heavy-body acrylic paints [S381].
- He started his practice with one generative sketch per day in October 2017 (Digital Inktober) in Processing [S381].

## In explainer work
- Sampling palettes from physical media is a transferable way to give explainer graphics a tactile, non-default look (see [[probabilistic-palette]]) [S381].
