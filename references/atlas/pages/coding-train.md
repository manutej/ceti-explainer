# The Coding Train

## Definition
The Coding Train is Daniel Shiffman's YouTube and web educational show on Processing/p5.js creative coding, begun on YouTube in 2015, with sequenced Tracks, one-off Challenges and live streams [S180][S384].

## Details
- The site separates Tracks (sequenced) from [[coding-challenges]] (one-off, building on Tracks, with prerequisites per page) [S182].
- The team credits distinct roles for editing/video production, illustrations/animations and content design [S180].
- Community: a Discord, a viewer Showcase and guides; support via YouTube memberships, Patreon and GitHub Sponsors; partners include the Processing Foundation and ml5.js [S181][S180].
- The YouTube channel lists topics including JavaScript basics, algorithmic art, machine learning, simulation and generative poetry [S183].
- Its p5.js 2.0 typography track covers variable fonts with animated `textWeight()`, `textContours()` and `textModel()` **[2.x]** [S46].
- The noise track's I.2 video contrasts `random()` with `noise()` and explains octaves [S87].
- natureofcode.com and thecodingtrain.com are built with Gatsby [S180][S179].

## In explainer work
- It is a live-coding show rather than post-produced explainer animation (researcher judgement), but its episode package (chapters, runnable web-editor sketch, remix showcase) is a reusable p5 explainer distribution model; see [[coding-train-episode-package]] [S386][S142].
- Its production workflow beyond team roles is undocumented [S180].
