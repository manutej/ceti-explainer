# Processing Foundation Fellowships

## Definition
The Processing Foundation Fellowship, running since 2013, funds individuals to build tools, curricula and tutorials; p5.js itself and the p5.js Editor originated in it [S280][S346].

## Details
- p5.js began as Lauren Lee McCarthy's 2013 fellowship [S346].
- Michael O'Connell (2020) ran weekly one-hour p5.js clubs for grades 3–6; nearly a quarter of students preferred p5.js over other child-friendly tools, and sessions moved to recorded Zoom in 2020 [S208].
- 2021 fellows included Angi Chau's teacher starter kit, Shawn Higgins's Scratch-to-p5 video tutorials, the Indigemoji p5.js+ml5.js prototype for the Arrernte language, and Pê Cinco (Portuguese reference plus short feature videos) [S212].
- Saskia Freeke's 2017 fellowship ran p5.js workshops for women, non-binary and femme-identifying people [S194].
- New backers announced in late Sep–Oct 2026 (Riot Games, Mark Cuban Foundation, Jerome Foundation) and a 2027 Gaming Fellowship track, an LA-based fellow co-designing a youth games toolkit [S280].
- In mid-2026 a staff statement called the 2026 Fellowship "under construction", with details planned for October [S281].

## In explainer work
- Fellowship outputs are a rich source of short p5 teaching videos and starter kits — e.g. Pê Cinco's feature videos and Higgins's tutorials [S212].
