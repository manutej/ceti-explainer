# Ben Fry

## Definition
Ben Fry co-founded [[processing]] with Casey Reas in 2001 and the [[processing-foundation]] in 2012, and co-authored [[getting-started-with-p5js]] [S344][S345][S353].

## Details
- Fry taught Processing's first workshop, in Japan, in 2001 [S344].
- Processing grew out of MIT's Aesthetics and Computation Group, founded by John Maeda in 1996, and Maeda's 1999 Design By Numbers [S344].
- The Processing Foundation was founded in 2012 by Reas, Fry and Shiffman [S345].

## In explainer work
- Processing's lineage is why so many loop-and-GIF explainers (Bees and Bombs, Etienne Jacob) are made in Processing rather than p5 — techniques port directly (see [[dave-whyte]], [[etienne-jacob]]) [S188][S185].
