# Patt Vira

## Definition
Patt Vira is a creative-coding educator whom p5js.org lists as publishing weekly p5.js tutorial videos, and author of a two-hour freeCodeCamp p5 course [S197][S189].

## Details
- The freeCodeCamp course (Feb 2024) is about two hours and builds five projects: rotating blocks, trigonometry patterns, 3D kinetic typography, hypnotic flowers and Mondrian-inspired generative art [S189].
- Each project introduces a new feature area — interaction, trigonometry, 3D type, loops, generative composition — a project-based structure rather than a reference walk [S189].
- Her YouTube About line is only a tagline about experimenting with code; video-level structure and workflow were not verified [S190].

## In explainer work
- Her course is a compact model for teaching explainer primitives (trig motion, [[kinetic-typography]]) through small finished pieces rather than exercises [S189].
- She belongs to the teacher-communicator group whose output is screen-captured live coding, not post-produced animation [S189][S197].
