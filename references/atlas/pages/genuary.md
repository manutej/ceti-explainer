# Genuary

## Definition
Genuary is the annual January daily-prompt generative-coding challenge; it is the most visible community form of the daily-constraint practice [S388][S242].

## Details
- Craig Kaplan did Genuary 2024 in p5.js via the web editor as a course catalogue, and reports that daily prompts create real time pressure; he abandoned the run after day 26 [S388].
- A Genuary 2026 participant repo used vanilla Canvas 2D with a p5-to-vanilla cheat sheet — not a p5 workflow example — exporting frames with toDataURL PNG and scaling low-res offscreen canvases for a pixel look [S242].
- Saskia Freeke's daily practice (since 2015) layers palette then monthly then weekly themes and publishes stills and GIFs [S195].
- No curated "best of Genuary in p5" source or p5 entry post-mortems were retrieved [S388][S242].

## In explainer work
- A daily-prompt run is a cheap way to build a library of motion beats reusable in explainers; Kaplan's catalogue model shows how to turn it into teaching material [S388].
