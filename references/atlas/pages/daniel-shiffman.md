# Daniel Shiffman

## Definition
Daniel Shiffman is an NYU ITP teacher, Processing Foundation co-founder and board member, host of [[coding-train]] and author of [[nature-of-code]] — the single largest p5.js teaching franchise [S180][S345][S384].

## Details
- He began making videos in 2012 and launched the Coding Train YouTube channel in 2015; the show mixes sequenced Tracks, one-off [[coding-challenges]] and live streams [S180].
- He co-founded the [[processing-foundation]] in 2012 with Reas and Fry [S345].
- The 2024 Nature of Code rewrite moved the book from Processing (2012, self-published) to p5.js, published by No Starch and free to read online [S384][S177].
- In 2019 he said YouTube ad revenue paid collaborators while his salary came from NYU, and framed his role as bridging the "last mile" between tools and learners [S385].
- p5js.org describes his teaching as covering variables, conditionals, loops, objects and arrays in p5.js, and the book as covering physics, generative algorithms and ML [S197].
- Key explainer-relevant episodes: Perlin noise ([[coding-challenge-24]]), Fourier epicycles ([[coding-challenge-130]]), 4D noise loops, and circle morphing via point resampling [S88][S221][S133][S341].

## In explainer work
- The Coding Train pipeline — build on camera, chapter the video, link a runnable web-editor sketch, host a remix showcase — is the best-documented p5 explainer distribution model; see [[coding-train-episode-package]] [S386][S142].
- His challenges are the canonical sources for [[shape-morph]] (vertex lerp) and [[noise-loop]] techniques used in explainer motion [S341][S133].
- He notes the web editor works poorly on mobile and tablet, a constraint for embedded explainers [S385].
