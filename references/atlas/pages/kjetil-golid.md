# Kjetil Golid

## Definition
Kjetil Golid is a generative artist reported to work mainly in JavaScript with p5.js, author of [[archetype]] on Art Blocks and of the chromotome palette library [S380][S379].

## Details
- His p5 use comes only from a crypto news site; the Art Blocks interview mentions only Processing [S380][S379].
- He has described moving from many random inputs toward systems that create complexity from few inputs, such as cellular automata and grammars [S379].
- He is reported to open-source most of his algorithms and keeps a personal palette library, chromotome [S380].

## In explainer work
- "Complexity from few inputs" is a useful explainer principle: fewer parameters make each visual change attributable and narratable (inference from [S379]).
