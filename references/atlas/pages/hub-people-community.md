# Hub: People, works and community

## Definition
This hub links every page in cluster C4: the people who build, teach and push p5.js; the books, courses and artworks they made; the institutions and community programs around them; the 2.x release line; and the atlas synthesis pages on the frontier and open questions [S180][S342][S345].

## Details
### Overview
- Practitioners split into teacher-communicators (screen-recorded live coding), loop-and-GIF artists from the Processing lineage, generative artists shipping hash-seeded works, and the maintainers who steward the tool [S180][S188][S374][S342].
- The best-documented p5 explainer distribution model is the Coding Train episode package; no mainstream post-produced explainer channel was verified as p5-animated [S386][S188].
