# Processing Foundation website (source site)

## Definition
**Processing Foundation website** — the nonprofit's own site: blog, release announcements, fellowships, grants and organisation history; operated by Processing Foundation. [S47]

Kind: **primary**; 23 registered sources, 23 rated primary. It is one of the source sites indexed in [[hub-sites]]. [S47][S63][S165]

## What it contributes
- More than 800 people have contributed to p5.js over 10+ years [S47][S274]
- 2026 OSS Microgrants funded p5.strands instancing (Akshat Patil) and translation of GLSL examples to p5.strands (Dorine Tipo) [S275][S294]
- No dollar amount was disclosed for Riot's support [S280]
- Freeke has made daily code-generated art since 2015-01-01 [S194]
- The Foundation says old p5.js-sound is no longer maintained. [S165]
- John Maeda founded MIT's Aesthetics and Computation Group in 1996 [S344]
