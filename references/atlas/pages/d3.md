# D3

## Definition

D3 is a low-level data-visualisation toolkit of about 30 modules that targets SVG and Canvas and has no concept of charts and no default presentation. [S409]

## Details

- Its data join (enter, update, exit) enables animated transitions between data states. [S409]
- The docs point to Observable Plot for common charts. [S409]
- No sourced head-to-head with p5 for explainer animation was found; the comparison is scope only. [S409]
- Maintenance 2026 and p5 2.x: independent of p5; not checked. [S409]

## In explainer work

Pick D3 when the explainer is data-driven and benefits from scales, axes and joins; pick p5 for freeform generative visuals, and drive D3 scales from the same t if combining (inference). [S409]
