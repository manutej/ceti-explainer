# p5.js project leadership

## Definition
p5.js is led by a small set of project leads and area stewards under the Processing Foundation; current lead is Kit Kuksenok, with stewards for WebGL/WebGPU, strands and tooling [S342][S50].

## Details
- Lauren Lee McCarthy was Creator and Lead 2013–2020; Qianqian Ye has been a lead since 2021 (on leave); Kit Kuksenok since 2024 [S342].
- Cassie Tarakajian created the p5.js Editor (2016–2022) [S342].
- Dave Pagurek leads much of p5.strands and WebGPU; Luke Plowden authored the strands intro tutorial; Perminder Singh focuses on the WebGL stack; Adam Ferriss co-wrote the framebuffer and GLSL tutorials [S47][S49][S50][S61][S64].
- The LGM 2026 strands talk was given by Pagurek, Plowden, Singh, Lim and Kuksenok [S50].
- p5.js describes itself as free, open-source and aimed at making sketching with code as intuitive as sketching in a notebook [S342].

## In explainer work
- Knowing who stewards which area tells you where to look for authoritative answers: Pagurek for GPU/export questions, Lim for add-ons and builds, Kuksenok for migration (see [[dave-pagurek]], [[kenneth-lim]], [[kit-kuksenok]]) [S50][S131].
