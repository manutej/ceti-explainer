# Zach Lieberman

## Definition
Zach Lieberman is an artist who has posted a code sketch daily for over ten years, co-created openFrameworks and teaches at MIT Media Lab [S393].

## Details
- He has made giclée prints of his sketches since 2018 [S393].
- His Box light studies launched as an Art Blocks auction in December 2024 [S393].
- He works in [[openframeworks]] per his bio; no source shows an official p5 port of his practice, so his relevance is method, not code [S393].

## In explainer work
- His decade of daily sketching is the strongest case that a daily practice builds a motion vocabulary transferable to explainers (see [[genuary]]) [S393].
