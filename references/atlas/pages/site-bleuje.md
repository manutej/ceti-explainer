# bleuje (Etienne Jacob) (source site)

## Definition
**bleuje (Etienne Jacob)** — the personal site of the loop-GIF artist, with FAQ and a looping tutorial; operated by Etienne Jacob. [S184]

Kind: **primary**; 3 registered sources, 3 rated primary. It is one of the source sites indexed in [[hub-sites]]. [S184][S185][S186]

## What it contributes
- Jacob exports MP4 for the main gallery and social media and offers a GIF gallery for smoother looping [S185]
- Jacob's offset examples include radial distance, linear (x+2y) offsets, spiral (distance plus angle) and noise offsets, and carry to 3D [S186]
- Jacob's main animation tool is Processing (Java); he also uses openFrameworks and occasionally GLSL shaders [S185]
