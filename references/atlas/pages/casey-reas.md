# Casey Reas

## Definition
Casey Reas co-founded [[processing]] with Ben Fry in 2001 and co-founded the [[processing-foundation]] in 2012; he co-wrote [[getting-started-with-p5js]] [S344][S345][S353].

## Details
- Reas and Fry began Processing as sketchbook notes in spring 2001; the first site went live on 20 October 2001 under the name Proce55ing [S344].
- Processing emerged from John Maeda's Aesthetics and Computation Group at MIT and was shaped by Design By Numbers' minimalism [S344].
- Processing Alpha shipped 2 February 2004, when the name changed to Processing [S344].
- Programs are called "sketches" because the solution emerges while writing — a framing p5.js inherits [S344].

## In explainer work
- The "sketch" metaphor Reas and Fry defined is the root of p5's setup/draw lifecycle that explainer engines wrap (see [[sketch-concept]]) [S344].
