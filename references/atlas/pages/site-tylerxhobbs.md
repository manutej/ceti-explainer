# Tyler Hobbs's website (source site)

## Definition
**Tyler Hobbs's website** — essays by the generative artist on flow fields, Fidenza, colour and probability distributions; operated by Tyler Hobbs. [S365]

Kind: **primary**; 5 registered sources, 5 rated primary. It is one of the source sites indexed in [[hub-sites]]. [S365][S366][S370]

## What it contributes
- Hobbs's color and triangle-subdivision essays use Quil/Clojure code. [S371][S372]
- Fidenza has seven scale settings, and Jumbo is the most common. [S365]
- Quantising flow angles to multiples of π/10 gives rocky forms, according to Hobbs. [S366]
