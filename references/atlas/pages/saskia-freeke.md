# Saskia Freeke

## Definition
Saskia Freeke is an artist and creative coder who has made daily code art since 1 January 2015 and ran p5.js workshops as a 2017 Processing Foundation fellow [S194].

## Details
- Her 2017 fellowship ran p5.js workshops for women, non-binary and femme-identifying people, with Code Liberation [S194].
- Her tools include Processing with the HYPE framework, Hexels, p5.js and Illustrator [S195].
- She iterates on existing code, layers palette then monthly then weekly themes, and publishes stills and GIFs on Tumblr and Instagram [S195].
- She recommends the p5.js reference and Coding Train tutorials to beginners [S195].
- The Smashing Magazine piece is attributed to her from first-person content; the byline was not explicitly confirmed [S195].

## In explainer work
- Her constraint-driven daily practice is the model behind [[genuary]]-style output: small daily iterations build a library of reusable motion ideas [S195].
