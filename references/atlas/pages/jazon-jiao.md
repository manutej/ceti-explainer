# Jazon Jiao

## Definition
Jazon Jiao is the author of [[manim-js]], a p5.js instance-mode recreation of 3Blue1Brown's animation engine, and of the YouTube explainer animations made with it [S328].

## Details
- Manim.js schedules objects with a `start` parameter given in frames, and each class's `show()` is called from `draw()` [S328].
- Its `setup2D` helper fixes 30 fps on a 1200×675 canvas [S328].
- It focuses on linear algebra and graph algorithms and holds the sources of its author's YouTube animations [S328].
- Its current maintenance status is unknown [S328].

## In explainer work
- Manim.js is the closest existing p5 port of a Manim-style scene engine and a reference for frame-scheduled object timelines (see [[manim]], [[timeline-builder]]) [S328].
