# Dave Whyte (Bees and Bombs)

## Definition
Dave Whyte, known as Bees and Bombs, is a theoretical-physics PhD who makes looping geometric GIFs in Processing; his grid-offset method is the most-copied loop technique in creative coding [S188].

## Details
- He holds a theoretical-physics PhD (foams and soap bubbles) from Trinity College Dublin [S188].
- He makes his GIFs in Processing, shares them on Tumblr and Twitter, and is supported via Patreon [S188].
- His standard technique repeats a simple element on a grid with timing that depends on grid position [S188].
- He sketches designs on paper before coding and prioritises visual intuition for mathematics over formal derivation [S188].
- Coding Challenge #86 recreates one of his loops (cube wave) in p5 WEBGL using oscillation and a position-based offset [S142].

## In explainer work
- The grid-offset method turns one simple motion into a propagating wave — ideal for signal-spreading or wavefront beats in explainers; see [[grid-offset-loop]] [S188][S142].
- Complexity should come from the offset field, not from more shape types [S188].
