# Controlled quotients

Batruin introduces hidden interior mediators whose raw states are nuisance
variables. Quotienting their coboundaries reduced the candidate budget from
2 000 to 1 000 per cluster. Aligning the hidden state removed the gap. Exact
CSP matched the quotient in every cluster.

Interpretation: the method is **invariance to the choice of stale
representative**, not superiority of cohomology over exact reasoning.

When proposing a quotient in sheaf-repair, name the nuisance field, re-run Φ
on the remaining overlaps, and record the move in the trace.
