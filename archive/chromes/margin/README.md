# E · THE MARGIN — the field notebook

A lab notebook on green-grey graph paper, written live by a physically simulated dip pen (iron-gall ink that
writes blue and dries blue-black, bleeding into the paper's fibres) and annotated in coloured pencil. The
viewer is a second hand: their predictions are written in graphite in the margin and stay on the page beside
the truth. It looks like someone thinking, not like software.

**Files.** `margin.glyphs.js` (from `tools/extract_glyphs.py`), `margin.kit.js` (glyphs, pen physics, bleed,
paper, bake, camera), two films. Build `python3 tools/make.py all`; renders `tools/final.sh`.

## The ladder
**Glance (built).** *Shared* (34.5 s): fifty runs as fifty full-width lines, longest first; the pen traces
the envelope through the line ends to a margin ruler where the viewer's pencil mark waits; a check carries the
same lines on (sage rings, doubled loops, overrun = cost). *Native* (27 s): pencil drafts weigh the words, ink
fair copies all look equally sure, drafts erased — which answer is wrong? — then the viewer's own guess.

**Grasp (2 min, spec).** Opens with THE TRACE as a notebook entry: "reconcile 40 invoices" as a numbered
procedure; `fetch_purchase_orders` pasted in as a printed slip; step 7 written then struck ("Acme Corp ≠ ACME
Corporation, totals mismatch"), a sage check in the margin, the retry by tax ID underneath, "✓ stop". The entry
collapses into *one* line of loops (the slip a doubled loop) and the page fills with fifty. Honesty footnotes in
pencil: "assumes independent slips — a shared bad source puts the same × in every row (sketch)"; "each check
costs a review minute — tallied beside the gates".

**Wield (live page).** Shared: both commits gate playback; your guess is written in your pencil and measured.
Native: the slider "times it read Sydney" re-runs counts → probability → the word chosen → the hand that
writes it. Next: a "place the checks" page — pencil a check after any of the 20 steps under a 3-check budget,
commit a prediction, then the pen writes fifty runs.

**Master (spec).** An explorable notebook: p, c, k, N as written values you cross out and rewrite (the strips
re-write); a calibration page plots all your guesses vs truth as a pencil scatter.

## Known weaknesses
- Row hairlines are pale at full view; the bead pattern carries the count, the curve carries the result.
- The commit frames are quiet paper with a hovering pencil.
- Native relies on toy counts (labelled sketch); the hidden "% sure" is the toy model's, not a real model's.
- The time-lapse (≈8 rows/s) is honest as time-lapse, not as handwriting.
