# E · THE MARGIN — notes

## Mark rule
Every mark is a pen or pencil gesture made at a time on this page: **one stroke = one thought written down.**
One continuous ink line of loops = one agent run; one loop = one step (the title sketch draws the loop
plan→act→observe→check once, large). A run that slips stops: the pen lifts mid-loop (the *address*) and a
peach-pencil × marks it; a finished run gets a sage ✓; a caught slip is a doubled loop (the retry) ringed in
sage. The viewer's guesses are written in **a second hand** (graphite, upright) — the margin is theirs. Only the
lab-book header is printed.

## Material
Green-grey engineering graph paper (8/40 px teal-grey grid), seeded fibre field, show-through of the previous
page (mirrored, 2 %), binding gutter. Iron-gall ink: written vivid blue, oxidises to blue-black while drying.
Coloured pencils (CETI hues, L/C re-tuned for paper) for annotations. No cream, hairline or vermilion.

## Depth rungs
1. Single-line glyphs derived from EMS Allure (author) / EMS Felix (viewer) (OFL) + hand-made ✓ × → ≈ ≠ ↺;
   per-glyph seeded slant/scale/baseline; letters joined into cursive paths, i-dots/t-bars deferred to word end.
2. Hand speed by the two-thirds power law v = K·κ^(−1/3); pen-up air travel.
3. Dynadraw spring-mass nib (≈26 Hz, ζ 0.72, stiffer for small writing); width from tip speed × stub-nib angle
   × pressure ramps; ink reservoir depletes → paler/thinner → re-dip (start blob).
4. Washburn bleed r = r∞·√(age/τ): halo + feathers along the fibre field; wet sheen; oxidation by age.
5. Dry strokes baked (sorted by dry time) into a 2× paper layer, snapshots for backward seeks; frame composed
   on a CPU canvas, one blit.
