# D · THE LEDGER IN MOTION — notes

## Mark rule
Every mark is a **unit posted to an account**: slip = one job (an agent reconciling 40 invoices in ten turns),
sheet = one invoice (Wield), hourglass = fixed hours (key on screen). Never scaled; more quantity = more units,
in Isotype rows of ten. Identity persists from opening to close; units move only when value moves. Jobs are whole;
hours are continuous, so a debit falling inside an hourglass *cuts* it (Neurath's partial symbol) and both parts
share one place.

## Material and type
CETI panel ground (#171B23); a ledger opened flat — the **spine is the gutter** (turn + tool called; in the native,
the statement). Facing pages are the twin worlds. Copper in process/claimed, sage clean/kept, peach exception or
failure cost, slate review time; the right gutter rule turns sage wherever a check stands. **Newsreader** (news text
face, tabular lining figures by default) for headline, deck and figures; **IBM Plex Sans Condensed** (tabular) for
heads and labels. 

## Depth
1. Pixel-grid pictograms (tear-off slip, knocked item lines, the total's double rule; struck-through void;
   dog-ear = a checker looked; hourglass with spent sand) → density-matched atlas, blitted; partial blits for cuts.
2. Re-packing by Hungarian assignment on Euclidean distance: the min-sum Euclidean matching is crossing-free, so
   units glide into holes and onto their address without tangling.
3. Twin pages on common random numbers: job r sits in mirrored slots and meets the same slip at the same turn.
4. Every figure is a count of units at t; balances under the double rule; expected ± sd dashed over the evidence.

## Doctrine
Impossibility: optimal re-packing of 100 identities on shared draws · belief/ruler: counts from units, block
heights, wing lengths · address: exception line · silence: no words needed to see which page won · thumbnail: a ledger. **Never:** icons in circles, KPI cards, gradients, glow, scaled symbols, fractional jobs, typed totals.

## Revision 1 (after JUROR + PEDAGOGY-CRIT)

check stood at every turn: rescued jobs are reversed out of the exception rows back into the block (dog-eared),
jobs caught early but failing later are re-filed down the page, and each line's review cost is debited as 2×
hourglasses in a margin on that line (cost as a mark). Folio figures are corrected the bookkeeper's way (old
struck in pencil, new in ink). (2) *Arntz-grade 2× pictograms*: three item rules, the total and its double rule,
tear-off foot; stamp = one check; hourglass = 20 h in **both** films. (3) *Commit beat in the material*: a pencil
